Design prototype only. Google sign-in is optional; guest reading stays local. Shelves never own or delete books. Ratings and journals are private. Downloads, reading positions and sync states are separate. The Reader demonstration uses Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 18, 28%; passage text is verified against Project Gutenberg 1342. No page counts or community ratings are invented. Scrollable content uses Android-style internal content areas.
